Definitions, sources and caveats
  • Attainment 8 adds up a pupil's grades across eight qualifications (English and maths count double), so 50 means an average of grade 5.
  • Disadvantaged pupils are those eligible for the pupil premium: eligible for free school meals in the last six years, looked after, or previously looked after.
  • EAL means a first language known or believed to be other than English. School-level figures for English-first-language pupils are worked out as the school total minus its EAL pupils.
  • SEND covers pupils with an EHC plan or on SEN support. Attainment for these pupils is published nationally, by region and by local authority, not school by school.
  • Offer is whether a school entered at least one pupil for the subject. Uptake is entries divided by pupils at the end of KS4. Entries are undiscounted, so a pupil entered twice counts twice.
  • Average grade covers GCSE 9 to 1 entries with U counted as 0 and entries with no result left out. Combined Science is the mean of its two grades. Vocational awards have no 9 to 1 grade.
  • Neither Progress 8 nor Key Stage 2 prior attainment is published for this cohort, whose Key Stage 2 tests in 2020 were cancelled.
  • Independent schools often enter qualifications that do not count in Attainment 8, so their scores are not comparable with state schools.
  • Local authority figures are for state-funded schools in each upper-tier authority, latest year. Attainment 8 measures are pupil-level figures from the Department for Education; the subject measures add up state-funded mainstream schools. City of London and the Isles of Scilly have too few pupils to publish. Ranks are among authorities with a figure. Boundaries: Office for National Statistics, December 2025.
  • Figures for the latest year are provisional. Small groups are suppressed by the Department for Education and appear blank.
  • Region comes from each school's local authority. IDACI fifth and Ofsted judgement come from Ofsted's management information as at 31 October 2026; fifth 1 is the least deprived. Ofsted's overall judgements ended in September 2024, so many schools have none.
